A research survey on how Named Entity Recognition can turn ROV pilots' spoken inspection notes into structured data. It spans the range from rule-based systems to BERT, and its setting is subsea pipeline inspection, where the aim is to cut the manual tagging involved.

The full survey is on ResearchGate: [Named Entity Recognition in Subsea Inspections](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/406006394_Named_Entity_Recognition_in_Subsea_Inspections).
