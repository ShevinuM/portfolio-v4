## About

I started my career as a software engineer in 2024 and have been in the industry for more than 2 years now. During this period the industry has considerably changed and I am in a constant pursuit of where I wanna be in 10 years. If you ask me what my dream is, it's being involved in theoretical fields that lead to science breakthroughs. 


## My Life

Growing up in Colombo, Sri Lanka, I really liked mathematics and even finished top of my school in it. So this pull towards problem solving led me to earning a BSc in Computer Science at Memorial University. Today I'm a software engineer at Enaimco building software for subsea integrity management. 

Throughout my childhood I've been obsessed with marine life. I still remember the first book I read about whales and how I've always had a deep connection to marine environments. I keep telling to my friends that I really want to dive with whales and to this date I haven't but hopefully I can one day. I recently read a book called 'How to Speak Whale' by Tom Mustill that opened my eyes to how I can use my foundation in Computer Science to learn how we can communicate with these animals. 80% of the ocean remains unexplored which makes me even more excited for the future.
